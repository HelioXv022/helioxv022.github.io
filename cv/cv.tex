\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Graduate Researcher, University of Waterloo and Perimeter Institute}{2024 -- 2026}
\begin{itemize}

        mutual information (CMI) as a diagnostic of how well neural networks can represent them.
  \item Implemented RBM and autoregressive RNN wavefunctions with variational Monte Carlo in NetKet and
        PyTorch; benchmarked them against exact diagonalization and MPS/DMRG (TeNPy).
  \item Wrote exact transfer-matrix code for CMI decay lengths from a bond-dimension-2 MPS, validated against
        brute-force state-vector calculations (agreement to 1e-12) and tested in CI.
  \item Manuscript in preparation: \textit{Conditional Information Flow and Neural Representability of 1D
        Rotated Cluster States}.
